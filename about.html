ABOUT AAZ

Professional travel,
personally handled.

AAZ Global Logistics & Autos provides pre-arranged chauffeur transportation for people who value timing, comfort, discretion and a clear plan for the road ahead.

THE SERVICE

A direct, human approach
to executive transport.

The experience is designed around pre-arranged journeys rather than on-demand rides. That means understanding the pickup, the destination, the schedule and the vehicle arrangement before the day begins.

AAZ is based in Abuja and can be requested for travel to destinations across Nigeria. Longer and cross-border journeys can also be considered when route and practical requirements allow.
