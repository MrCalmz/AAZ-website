This page describes what the website does with information you enter into the booking form.

Booking information

The current static website processes booking details in your browser to prepare a message. It does not store those details in a website database.

WhatsApp

When the AAZ WhatsApp number is configured, submitting the form opens WhatsApp with the booking message you prepared. WhatsApp then handles the message under its own service and privacy practices.

Third-party services

This version does not intentionally include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. It uses Google Fonts for typography. Any future analytics or booking integrations should be reflected in this notice before launch.

Questions

For questions about information shared during a booking request, use the business contact details provided by AAZ.
