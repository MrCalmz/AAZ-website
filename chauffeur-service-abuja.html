CHAUFFEUR SERVICE · ABUJA

A private driver
for the day, or the journey.

Pre-arranged chauffeur transportation in Abuja for business, airport travel, hotel movement, events, private appointments and longer road journeys.

01 / WHAT THE SERVICE IS

Travel with the vehicle and schedule arranged in advance.

AAZ Global Logistics Ventures & Autos provides a direct chauffeur arrangement rather than an on-demand ride-hailing experience. The journey is planned around the pickup, destination, timing, passengers and vehicle requirement.

For visitors and residents in Abuja, this is useful for airport arrivals, business meetings, hotel transfers, dinners, events, private appointments and travel to other cities.

02 / WHO USES IT

Built for schedules that need more attention.

Executives, visiting professionals, families, event organisers and private travellers can request a chauffeur. Longer itineraries can be discussed around the full schedule.

03 / TRAVEL BEYOND ABUJA

Your destination does not have to be in the capital.

AAZ can be requested for interstate travel across Nigeria. Routes can include Abuja to Lagos, Kaduna, Kano, Jos and other destinations, subject to the specific booking and practical requirements.
